# Changelog

Every version of the ATLANTECH brand kit. The brand owner adds an entry here whenever a version is sent out.

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-25)

First release.

- **Logos:** 12 transparent variants rebuilt from the original identity files. The primary logo is the full-colour lockup.
- **Colour:** 5 brand palette colours, plus 21 semantic UI tokens in light and dark mode.
- **Type:** Montserrat for display and Inter for text, in 15 text styles.
- **Platforms:** Flutter kit for mobile, web kit (CSS, Tailwind, Vue) for the dashboard, and a design kit (Figma tokens, swatches, references) for UI/UX.
